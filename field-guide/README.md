# Operator Field Guide

Practical notes from an experienced SUB/WAVE operator.

The Field Guide is deliberately different from the official manual. The manual
explains how a setting works; these notes explain how choices play out over
weeks of listening: what makes a DJ brief useful, how to give a show an identity
without making its music pool brittle, and when restraint produces the better
radio moment.

presenting one station's taste as the only valid way to programme SUB/WAVE.
